Research basis for C3 technology-stack scoring

1) Putra, J. A., Rakhman, T., & Biddinika, M. K. (2019).
   Selection between AHP and TOPSIS for Academic Information Systems Decision Making Model.
   Proceedings of ICASESS, pp. 86-89. DOI: 10.5220/0009878700860089.
   Relevance: compares software alternatives using multiple weighted criteria and ranks the results.

2) Liu, K. C., Kehoe, D. F., & Li, D. (2008).
   A FAHP-Based Technology Selection and Specification Methodology.
   Relevance: treats IT technology selection as a multi-criteria system-design decision and uses weighted AHP/FAHP evaluation of alternatives.

How these support C3:
They support the research method of defining candidates, evaluating them against several criteria, applying explicit weights, and ranking the alternatives deterministically.

Important accuracy note:
The papers do not prescribe C3's exact weights of 0.35, 0.25, 0.20, and 0.20. The repository itself describes those values as a provisional project policy. Present them as researcher-defined weights inspired by multi-criteria decision-making literature and requiring expert validation or sensitivity analysis.
